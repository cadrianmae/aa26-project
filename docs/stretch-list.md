# Stretch list

Deferred, not cut. Pick these up **only** once the artefact is submittable:
built, README complete, video recorded.

Ordered by marks-per-hour, best first.

| # | Item | Why deferred | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sound effects — weapon fire, unit death, harvest tick | Music alone already satisfies the audio criterion; effects deepen it | 1 h |
| 2 | Factory upgrades, five tuned tiers | The economy loop is what is marked, not the upgrade curve. Ships as one scaling factor | 1 h |
| 3 | `CommanderAI` tuning pass | It needs to read as autonomous, not to be competitive | 1-2 h |
| 4 | Enemy swarm parity with the player's | Smaller enemy swarm is cheaper to run and easier to read on video | 30 min |
| 5 | Split and merge as player-triggered actions | Currently emergent only, which already demonstrates the idea | 2 h |
| 6 | Obstacle avoidance, five-feeler | Nothing to avoid yet; needs obstacles first | 2 h |
| 7 | Procedural animation on the units | Genuine Complexity-band evidence, but the wireframe hulls read fine static | 3 h+ |

## The rule

Nothing on this list is worth a broken submission. The video is recorded from
a build, the README carries a third of the marks, and neither is recoverable
after the deadline. If picking one of these up would put either at risk, it
waits.

## If time appears

Most likely window is Tuesday, after the build is exported, the video is
uploaded and the README is complete. At that point items 1 and 2 are the best
value: both are small, both are visible in a video that has already been
recorded once and can be re-recorded cheaply.
